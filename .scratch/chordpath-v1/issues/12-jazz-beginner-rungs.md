# 12: Jazz beginner rungs

**What to build:** Jazz rungs 2 to 5 in the month's key, playable in Wait and Flow. Rung 2: major and minor triads in all inversions, diagram shown, pitch classes graded. Rung 3: chord shells (root, 3rd, 7th) on a 2-5-1. Rung 4: half-note bass over a 2-5-1, roots and fifths then chromatic approaches, graded as the bass pitch class per half bar. Rung 5: chord-tone targeting, the user improvises over a 2-5-1 with click and bass while the app scores each bar by the share of heard energy on chord tones, pass at a curriculum threshold. Intermediate and advanced tiers appear as locked placeholders with rung names only.
