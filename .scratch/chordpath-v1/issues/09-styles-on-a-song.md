# 09: Styles on a song

**What to build:** Church rungs 5 to 8: Charlie Tran's four Styles over one of the user's songs. The Song screen shows the song's current Style rung and a Practice button. Style 1: top note plus bass, the top note and bass are shown, chord tones graded. Style 2: top note plus left-hand comping. Style 3: right-hand comping plus left-hand bass, with the rhythm pattern shown. Style 4: functional-level spread voicings with 7ths, the voicing generator picks notes below a given top note. Each Style runs in Wait then Flow over the whole Chart, with sections labeled and cues shown. Passing Flow advances the song's Style rung. A Reference page explains each Style and each comping rhythm in a few sentences, in the app's own words, crediting Charlie Tran and Piano With Jonny as sources without reproducing their materials.

**Blocked by:** 06 Flow mode, 07 Church rungs 2 to 4, 08 Songs by hand
